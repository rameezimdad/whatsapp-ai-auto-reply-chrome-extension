How to install the WhatsApp AI auto reply extension

About 10 minutes. You need Chrome on a computer, a licence key and an AI key.

Install

  1. Download the zipwa-ai-reply-v1.12.1.zip from GitHub.
  2. Extract itRight-click, Extract All, and keep the folder somewhere safe like D:\WA AI Reply.
  3. Open the Extensions pageType chrome://extensions in the address bar.
  4. Turn on Developer modeThe switch is at the top right.
  5. Load unpackedSelect the extracted folder, the one with manifest.json inside.
  6. Pin itClick the puzzle icon and pin WA AI Reply to the toolbar.

Set up

  1. Activate your licence keyPaste the key on the License page and press Activate.
  2. Add your AI keyGemini has a free tier at aistudio.google.com. ChatGPT, Claude and Grok work too.
  3. Fill in Business infoPick your business type, then add products, prices, timings and delivery.
  4. Test the AIAsk a question the way a customer would. Nothing goes to WhatsApp from there.
  5. Open WhatsApp WebScan the QR code and click the button at the bottom left so it reads AI Reply ON.
  6. Check and sendEach reply waits in the Reply to check panel. Press Send or Skip.

Frequently asked questions

About AI auto reply on WhatsApp Web.

How do I auto reply to WhatsApp messages with AI?

Install WA AI Reply in Chrome, activate your licence key, add an AI key and your business info, then open WhatsApp Web and turn AI Reply on. The AI writes a reply to each new customer message and you press Send.

Can I use ChatGPT for WhatsApp auto reply?

Yes. Pick ChatGPT in AI engine and paste your OpenAI API key. Claude, Gemini and Grok work the same way.

Does it work with WhatsApp Business?

Yes. It works with any account you open in WhatsApp Web, including WhatsApp Business accounts.

Is it a WhatsApp AI chatbot or AI agent?

It works like an AI agent for your WhatsApp: it answers from your business info and knowledge base, remembers each customer, takes orders and can check live data through MCP tools. By default you approve every reply before it goes out.

Can it reply in Urdu or Roman Urdu?

Yes. It replies in the language the customer writes in, including Urdu, Roman Urdu, Arabic, Hindi and English, or in the language you set for that customer.

Can I use Gemini for free?

Google gives a free tier for Gemini API keys at aistudio.google.com. The extension itself needs a paid licence key.

Is my chat data sold or stored on your server?

No. Settings, chats and customer notes stay in your browser. Messages go only to the AI provider you picked. The licence server sees only your licence key and a random device code. Read the privacy policy.

How do I install a Chrome extension from a zip file?

Extract the zip to a folder, open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and select the folder that has manifest.json inside.
